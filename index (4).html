Print center

Choose a paper format. Only labels will be printed; the dashboard and controls are excluded.
A5 on A4: each label is 148.5 × 210 mm. Two labels are arranged side by side on A4 landscape paper, so you can cut the sheet in half. In the print dialog, select A4 and Landscape.

Paper & layout

Thermal mode prints one label per 100 × 150 mm page. In the browser print dialog, choose the matching custom paper size if it isn't detected automatically.

Print checklist

01
Select the correct paper
A4 landscape for two A5 labels, or 100 × 150 mm for thermal.
02
Set scale to 100%
Turn off browser headers and footers. Avoid “Fit to page” if it changes the label size.
03
Test one sheet first
Check the PIN code, address edges, and printer orientation before printing a batch.
